32.2 Honest reading of the numbers

  1. “Confirmed” here means exact in analysis or in simulation, not measured on a device. No sub-measure of Ch. 7–31 has tested on hardware as its primary status. The only row that cites hardware as a caveat is readout_drag_affine (Ch. 25, §168 of the notes). The device-measured advantages of the original treatise live in RESEARCH_NOTES.md and are neither reproduced nor extended here.
  2. Of the 82 exact rows, 18 carry a caveat (mostly hardware unverified): the result is correct, but its transfer to a device was not verified. The remaining 64 have no caveat, and several of them are identities (for example, \(F\preceq4g\) with \(P=8\), or exact \(N\) mod 4 for \(N=2,n=4\)).
  3. Zero measured gain means that no chapter of this rewrite measures a gain against a classical or standard method on real data; the “without qang vs with qang” comparisons are of resources, of analytic exactness or of simulations.
