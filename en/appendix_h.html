Appendix H — Catalogue of qang sub-measures

The qang unit does not reformulate any physical law. Each sub-measure in this catalogue is a reading of one probability, or of a few, and equals a quantity that physics or statistics already has. What a sub-measure can bring is a decision: fewer shots, a shorter pulse, a compilation chosen before running, a reading that noise does not bias. For each one the catalogue records its definition, what it equals, which decision it improves and with what evidence, and classifies it with one of the statuses of Table H.1.

The tables are generated from the registry qang.measures with tools/make_apendice_h.py, the same source for both languages of the book. A new sub-measure enters the catalogue as unverified and changes status only with a test, preferably pre-registered (Chapter 28).

H.8 Balance

Of 52 sub-measures, 18 are exact identities, 14 have a measured gain in simulation or analysis, 3 were tested on a real device (with a mixed result for the milli-qang), 7 are mixed, 4 turned out equivalent to the usual practice, 2 lost to it, 3 are unverified and 1 was not adopted. The gains do not come from the unit but from the decisions the reading allows before spending resources: choosing the minimum-area representative, the compilation of least exposure, the code from two readings, or reading only the weight sector that T1 does not contaminate. The unverified sub-measures are the list of pending experiments; the catalogue keeps them in sight so that they are not cited as results.

H.9 In code

from qang import measures

measures.summary()                       # sub-measures per status
measures.by_status("sin_verificar")      # the pending experiments
m = measures.find("min_area")
m.decision[1], m.evidence[1]             # the decision it improves and the evidence, in English
measures.resolve(m)                      # qang.pulse_area.minimal_area_circuit
