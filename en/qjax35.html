Chapter 35 — First Real-Hardware Runs: IQM Garnet (Neutrinos §165, Milli-qang §168, E=mc² §170) Recomputed from the Stored Files

Updated: November 16, 2026

Rewrite of the hardware chapter on the basis of Ch. 1–34. Module: qang.qgarnet (NumPy; neither iqm-client nor credits are needed). Verification: tools/verify_cap35.py, which recomputes everything from examples/data/iqm_* and compares the pre-registrations with the git history. Status: tested on hardware, with the scope of a single run on a single day on one chip. There is no replication. Abstract. Three pre-registered runs on IQM Garnet are recomputed from the stored counts: the weight filter lowers the three-flavour neutrino error 1.59× and moves the oscillation minimum from 0.8 to 2.6 GeV; the milli-qang run gives a readout from two poles (\(e_0=0.0055\), \(e_1=0.029\)), an X-gate cost of 1.64 m-qg, and one failed prediction (M2). Each pre-registration hash matches the script blob at its commit. Single runs, one day: no replication.

35.1 Reading rules

  1. Every number comes from a stored file of the run and from a function of qang.qgarnet. What comes only from simulations or from the notes is marked unverified.
  2. m-qg \(=1000\,qg_Z\) (reading); qga is the angular scale (1 turn = 1000 qga). Only m-qg appears here.
  3. A failed prediction is recorded as failed.

35.2 What is taken to the chip

The reading of a real qubit is that of Ch. 1: \(qg_Z=2p_0-1\). What surrounds it changes: compilation to native gates, its own readout errors and per-shot credits. qang contributes three things, all in post-processing: the weight filter (if the circuit conserves it), the witness (fraction kept) and the reading in m-qg. The readout correction is affine:

\[m' = (1-e_0-e_1)\,m + 1000\,(e_1-e_0),\qquad e_0=P(1\mid 0),\ e_1=P(0\mid 1).\]

predicted_raw_mqg applies it and correct_mqg inverts it.

35.4 Milli-qang on one qubit (§168)

Job 01a167ab, 4000 shots per circuit, one qubit. The two poles give \(e_0=0.0055\), \(e_1=0.0290\) and contrast \(0.9655\) (readout_from_poles, contrast).

target (m-qg) raw reading − predicted (m-qg) \(z\)
+707 \(-15.6\) \(-1.34\)
+500 \(-32.2\) \(-2.29\)
−500 \(-55.2\) \(-3.91\)
−707 \(-27.9\) \(-2.26\)
0 (pooled equator) \(+1.5\) \(+0.25\)
  • M1 (at least 4 of 5 within \(3\sigma\)): met, but not cleanly: the four points of the sweep read below the prediction and the −500 one sits at \(3.9\sigma\).
  • M2 (dispersion of the equator copies between 0.55 and 1.45 of the binomial \(\sigma\)): failed, with \(0.500\). The 10 copies spread half as much as shot noise allows. It remains unexplained; none is proposed.
  • M3/M4 (chains of \(k\) X gates): the deficit goes from \(-2.6\) m-qg (\(k=3\)) to \(+63.2\pm7.7\) m-qg (\(k=41\)), \(8.2\sigma\). The slope per X gate, fitted through the origin with \(k-1\) gates, is 1.64 m-qg (\(\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=0.81\)). M3 passes, but the prediction’s design makes it poorly informative beyond \(k=15\) (recorded that way in the run).

35.5 E=mc² as a reading (§170)

Job 01a19c58, 2000 shots per circuit, 24 circuits on one qubit. The poles give \(e_0=0.0030\), \(e_1=0.0265\) (contrast \(0.9705\)). The 16 corrected readings of the sweep are recomputed from the counts (correct_mqg) and agree with the stored analysis; they differ from the exact curve between \(-78.9\) and \(+38.6\) m-qg (a mix of gate error and shot noise, \(\sigma\) from 4 to 17 m-qg). The stored verdict is 5 of 5. That verdict is read from the file; its thresholds are defined in examples/iqm_zitterbewegung_qg.py.

35.7 Credit budget

Initial balance 30. Tests \(0.50+0.50\); runs \(0.50+16.00\) (the cost of the neutrino one is the difference of two balances, it is not written). Spent 17.50 (tests 3.3 %, runs 55.0 %), balance 12.50. The §170 run (≈7.7 credits, estimated with the §168 rate) leaves ≈4.8. The arithmetic is exact (budget_summary); the credit figures come from the notes.

35.8 What this chapter does not claim

  1. There is no replication: each run is one day, one chip, one qubit (or 3). The 5-qubit, two-day replication is prepared (examples/iqm_milliqang_replication_qg.py) and not run.
  2. “The chip was better than its model” (raw error \(0.062\) versus \(0.126\) of the simulated Garnet model; factor \(1.59\) versus \(2.16\)) uses simulations that were not re-run here: unverified.
  3. qang.iqm (readout correction and chain choice) was written after the runs and was not tested on the real chip; it requires iqm-client. The runs were made with the scripts of examples/ and qang.interop.
  4. IQM’s compilation advantage (12 CZ versus 18 two-qubit gates on IBM) was an optimization-level effect (3 versus 1), not a chip effect: at the same level they give the same. That count requires Qiskit and was not re-run here (unverified).

from qang.qgarnet import readout_from_poles, contrast, predicted_raw_mqg, correct_mqg, fit_slope_origin, neutrino_summary, load
def test_readout_roundtrip_and_poles():
    e0, e1 = readout_from_poles({"0": 3978, "1": 22}, {"1": 3884, "0": 116})
    assert abs(e0 - 0.0055) < 1e-12 and abs(e1 - 0.029) < 1e-12 and abs(contrast(e0, e1) - 0.9655) < 1e-12
    for t in (-1000.0, -707.0, 0.0, 500.0):
        assert abs(correct_mqg(predicted_raw_mqg(t, e0, e1), e0, e1) - t) < 1e-9
def test_stored_neutrino_run():
    S = neutrino_summary(load("examples/data/iqm_neutrino_qg_run.json"))
    assert abs(S["factor"] - 1.5929) < 1e-3 and S["emin_filtered"] == 2.6 and S["emin_raw"] == 0.8
