27.1 XY conserves the weight: the quantum walk is a sequence of RBS

\(H_{XY}=J\sum_k(X_kX_{k+1}+Y_kY_{k+1})/2\) commutes with the Hamming weight; in the weight-1 sector it is the adjacency matrix \(J A_n\) (continuous-time quantum walk of one exciton). The propagator of one link is exactly an RBS (Givens) gate of angle \(Jt\) — the same gate as the unary loader (Ch. 23) — so the even-odd Trotter step is an RBS network with \(\Sigma p=1\) exact, and the symmetry filter of Ch. 24 applies unchanged to the dynamics. First-order Trotter error (\(n=6\), \(J=1\), \(t=2\)): \(0.317\) (1 step, RBS of \(318\) qga), \(0.111\) (4), \(0.029\) (16), \(0.0073\) (64 steps, RBS of \(5.0\) qga) ∝ \(dt\) (exact). Per-site reading \(qg_Z^{(k)}=1-2p_k\): \([998,735,168,366,787,947]\) m-qg at \(t=2\). XY dynamics in the weight-1 sector is a Givens/RBS network: weight conservation is exact, the symmetry filter transfers verbatim, and first-order Trotter error scales as \(dt\).

27.2 Affine Bloch channels: a single calibration (M,c)

Every 1-qubit channel is \(\mathbf r\mapsto M\mathbf r+\mathbf c\) (12 numbers). \(T_1\): \(M=\mathrm{diag}(\sqrt{1-\gamma},\sqrt{1-\gamma},1-\gamma)\), \(\mathbf c=(0,0,\gamma)\) (matches Kraus to \(10^{-14}\)); dephasing: \(M=\mathrm{diag}(1-2p,1-2p,1)\); readout (Ch. 25): \(M_{zz}=1-e_{01}-e_{10}\), \(c_z=e_{10}-e_{01}\). Composition is another pair \((M,\mathbf c)\): \(T_1(0.2)\circ\text{dephasing}(0.05)\circ\text{readout}(0.02,0.06)\) takes \((0.6,0,0.5)\to(0.483,0,0.592)\), \(+500\to+592\) m-qg, \(qg_\tau\) \(0.624\to0.645\). Rule: calibrate the whole chain as a single affine map (12 parameters) instead of three models, and use \(qg_\tau\) (Ch. 5) as the purity invariant (exact).

27.3 One-qubit varQTE: McLachlan with RY(θ) in qga

\(H=\omega Z/2\) from \(|+\rangle\): \(qg_Z(\tau)=-\tanh(\omega\tau)\) (exact). With the ansatz \(R_Y(\theta)|0\rangle\), the McLachlan equation is \(\dot\theta=\omega\sin\theta\) (metric \(g=1/4\) in rad, \((2\pi/1000)^2/4\) in qga); explicit Euler converges as \(1/\text{steps}\): error \(9.2\cdot10^{-3}\) (30), \(9.2\cdot10^{-5}\) (3000), final \(-995\) m-qg. It is the thermal state of Ch. 11 (\(qg_Z=\tanh\)) as a fixed point: 1 parameter, 0 ancillas, no plateaus (\(P=1\)). For \(P>1\) apply Ch. 9 (regularized \(F_Z\) metric), never plain descent in \(qg_Z\) (THEORY_CORE §2.1).
