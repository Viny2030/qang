Chapter 50 — Barren plateaus and training limits: Jacobian, Fisher and shot bounds

exact | unverified — Training and sampling synthesis. The plateau diagnostic and shot bounds are verifiable calculations; extrapolation to a specific device remains unverified.

50.1 The plateau as a diagnostic (exact)

qang.qfisher.gradient_variance measures the variance of \(\partial L/\partial\theta_0\) with parameters uniform in \([0,1000)\) qga: a barren plateau appears as variance near zero. It is a training diagnostic, not a claim about the physics of the problem. qang.qfisher.circuits_for_metric separates the QFI cost via Hadamard tests from the CFI\(_Z\) cost via parameter shift.

50.2 Jacobian and natural step (exact)

For the real single-qubit state, \(q=qg_Z=\cos\theta\) and \(dq/d\theta=-\sin\theta\). The inverse diverges at the poles; inverse_jacobian_clipped and inverse_jacobian_tikhonov regularize that singularity. The metric \(F_q=1/(1-q^2)\) explains why the natural gradient in \(q\) equals plain gradient descent in \(\theta\) to first order.

from qang.gradients import inverse_jacobian_clipped, natural_gradient_step_qg, signed_qg_step
from qang.qlimits import hoeffding_shots, sufficient_shots_entropy

inverse_jacobian_clipped(0.0)
natural_gradient_step_qg(0.5, 1.0, 0.1)
signed_qg_step(0.5, 1, 0.1, 0.1)
hoeffding_shots(0.05, 0.01)
sufficient_shots_entropy(2, 0.05, 0.01)

qang.gradients.pole_damping_factor scales the step by \(|\sin\theta|\): it slows down near the poles, where little coherence is available, and does nothing on the equator.

50.3 Sign and pole crossing (exact)

\(qg_Z\) alone covers \([0,\pi]\); the pair \((qg_Z,s)\) with \(s=\operatorname{sign}(qg_X)\) covers the full circle. signed_qg_step reflects the excess when the step crosses a pole and flips the branch sign. It is the continuation of the same path, not an optimizer restart.

50.4 Shot bounds (exact for the bound; unverified on hardware)

hoeffding_shots and sufficient_shots_entropy give deliberately loose distribution-free bounds for the mean and entropy. qang.qlimits.qgs_per_qubit, qang.qlimits.total_correlation_z and qang.qlimits.purity_range_from_z relate those bounds to entanglement and purity as seen from \(Z\). Bounds do not replace noise calibration nor guarantee that an observed plateau is physical.
