22.1 Energy and ergotropy of a cell in qg

With \(H=\omega|1\rangle\langle1|\): \(E/\omega=(1-qg_Z)/2\) and, with \(r\) the local Bloch radius (Ch. 5), \[\frac{W}{\omega}=\frac{r-qg_Z}{2},\qquad \text{passive fraction}=\frac{1-r}{1-qg_Z}.\] Full charge \(qg_Z=-1\) (\(-1000\) m-qg): \(W=\omega\). Finding: the \(Z\) reading alone does not determine the extractable charge: \(|+\rangle\) (\(0\) m-qg, \(r=1\)) has \(W=\omega/2\); \(I/2\) (\(0\) m-qg, \(r=0\)) has \(W=0\). Ergotropy requires \(r\) (3 bases or unbiased squares, THEORY_CORE §1), just like \(qg_\tau\) (exact). Collective versus parallel charging power: unverified here (no advantage is claimed). Ergotropy is \((r-qg_Z)/2\) in units of \(\omega\); the \(Z\) readout alone cannot distinguish a coherent half-charged cell from a passive one.

22.2 Networks: swapping multiplies visibilities

A chain of \(k\) Werner links \(V\) with ideal Bell measurements: the composite channel is depolarizing with \(V_{\text{end}}=V^k\) (verified numerically \(0.9\times0.8=0.720\): \(qg_{ZZ}=720\) m-qg). Utility (Ch. 15): \(V^k>1/3\) ⇒ \(k_{\max}=\lfloor\ln(1/3)/\ln V\rfloor\): \(V=0.99\to109\) links (\(334\) m-qg), \(0.95\to21\), \(0.9\to10\) (\(349\) m-qg; the 11th falls to \(314\)), \(0.8\to4\). Network planning: measure \(qg_{ZZ}\) per link (1 basis), multiply, compare with \(333\) m-qg (exact for Werner; non-Werner states: upper bound, Ch. 15 §4). Loss per link: rate \(\times\eta^k\) (Ch. 16), not fidelity.
