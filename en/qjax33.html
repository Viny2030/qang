Chapter 33 — String Theory: What qang Measures Can and Cannot Say (the XX Chain as a c = 1 Free Boson)

Día de análisis y actualización: 23 de noviembre de 2026

Analysis and update date: November 23, 2026

Chapter added on the basis of Ch. 1–32 (Ch. 5 and 7: \(qg_S\) and \(mi_Z\) are not entanglement measures; Ch. 18: scramblers; Ch. 26 and 27: correlations and XY). Module: qang.qcft (exact NumPy). Verification: tools/verify_cap33.py. Short answer to “are qang measures useful for string theory?”: not as a test of the theory; yes as a readout language for a toy model (a 1+1-dimensional CFT, the free boson), with limits that this chapter measures.

33.1 Scope: the only thing claimed

A bosonic string has a worldsheet described by a 1+1-dimensional conformal field theory (CFT); a compact free boson is the basic building block, with central charge \(c=1\). An XX spin chain (or free fermions on a lattice) is a lattice realization of that free boson. That is all this chapter takes from string theory: a lattice model whose continuum limit is a CFT with \(c=1\). No strings, gravity, extra dimensions, spectrum, T-duality or AdS/CFT are simulated: those claims remain not adopted (§33.7). The bosonic string requires matter with \(c=26\) to cancel the anomaly; the \(c=1\) chain neither satisfies it nor pretends to.

33.2 Model

Open chain of \(n\) sites, \(H=-\sum_i(c_i^\dagger c_{i+1}+\text{h.c.})\) with \(N=n/2\) fermions (≡ XX chain via Jordan–Wigner). The ground state is a Slater determinant and everything follows from \(C_{ij}=\langle c_i^\dagger c_j\rangle\). qang readings: \(qg_{Z,i}=1-2C_{ii}\) (m-qg \(=1000\,qg_Z\)) and connected \(qg_{ZZ}=\langle Z_iZ_j\rangle-\langle Z_i\rangle\langle Z_j\rangle=-4C_{ij}^2\) (Wick).

33.3 Exact identities

For \(n=8\) the full state of the XX chain (256 amplitudes, \(N=4\) sector) and the computation with \(C_{ij}\) agree: the connected \(qg_{ZZ}\) to \(10^{-12}\) on three pairs \((1,2),(2,5),(0,7)\) and the block entropy to \(10^{-10}\) for \(\ell=2,4\) (exact). Example: \(qg_{ZZ}^{c}(1,2)=-244.84\) m-qg and \(S(4)=0.8220\) bits.

33.4 Central charge: c → 1

The entropy of a block of \(\ell\) sites at the edge of an open chain is \(S(\ell)=\frac c6\log_2\!\big[\tfrac{2n}{\pi}\sin\tfrac{\pi\ell}{n}\big]+s_0\). Fitting with even \(\ell\) between \(n/8\) and \(n/2\):

\(n\) 32 64 128 256 512
fitted \(c\) 1.1165 1.0648 1.0337 1.0171 1.0086
\(\lvert c-1\rvert\) 0.1165 0.0648 0.0337 0.0171 0.0086

The error halves each time \(n\) doubles (∝ \(1/n\)): it converges to \(c=1\) (exact, numerical). Note: the entropy is computed with the full correlation matrix \(C_A\), not with a qang reading.

33.5 What qang does see: the power law

The connected \(qg_{ZZ}\) of two sites at odd distance \(r\) is \(-4/(\pi r)^2\) in the infinite chain (scaling dimension 1). In open chains the log-log slope of \(\lvert qg_{ZZ}^c\rvert\) versus \(r\in\{3,\dots,31\}\) is \(-1.907\) (\(n=400\)), \(-1.952\) (\(800\)), \(-1.976\) (\(1600\)), toward \(-2\); at the center with \(r=5\): \(-16.37\) m-qg versus \(-4000/(5\pi)^2=-16.21\) m-qg (exact; the residual is an edge effect and decreases with \(n\)). It is a two-point reading: it costs \(O(n^2)\) pairs and gives only \(\lvert C_{ij}\rvert\), not the phase.

33.6 What qang does not see: local readings

At the center of the chain with \(n=128\): \(\lvert qg_Z\rvert\le1.8\cdot10^{-12}\) m-qg and \(qg_S=1.00000\) bits at all sites (particle-hole symmetry fixes \(C_{ii}=1/2\)). The block entropy, by contrast, grows from 0.773 (\(\ell=2\)) to 1.575 bits (\(\ell=64\)). A local reading is identical for \(\ell=2\) and \(\ell=64\): neither \(qg_Z\) nor \(qg_S\) can give \(c\) (against, consistent with Ch. 5, 7 and 18: \(qg_S\) is not an entanglement measure).

33.7 What this chapter does not say

  1. It does not test string theory: reproducing \(c=1\) and an exponent \(-2\) in a spin chain is a property of the free-boson CFT, which also appears in a Heisenberg chain, in a Luttinger liquid and in many models unrelated to strings.
  2. There is no evidence that the qg or qga unit simplifies anything in string theory: no quantity of the theory was found that is better expressed in m-qg (not adopted).
  3. AdS/CFT, T-duality, string spectrum, compactification, strings with \(c=26\): unverified/not adopted; there is no code or verifier.
  4. The relation to chapters 17–18 (black holes as scramblers) is one of quantum-information language, not a holographic verification.

tests/test_qcft.py

import numpy as np
from qang.qcft import correlation_matrix, connected_qgzz, exact_state_xx, spin_zz_connected, block_entropy_bits, spin_block_entropy_bits, fit_central_charge, local_qgz
def test_identities():
    n = 6; C = correlation_matrix(n); psi = exact_state_xx(n)
    assert abs(connected_qgzz(C, 1, 3) - spin_zz_connected(psi, n, 1, 3)) < 1e-12
    assert abs(block_entropy_bits(C, 3) - spin_block_entropy_bits(psi, n, 3)) < 1e-10
def test_central_charge(): assert abs(fit_central_charge(128)[0] - 1.0) < 0.05
def test_local_flat(): assert np.max(np.abs(local_qgz(correlation_matrix(64)))) < 1e-10

F2 — XX-chain central charge and the limit of local qg readout

Hypothesis H2 (unverified). Local qg readings cannot detect the central charge; emergent geometry is not verifiable with local qg (unverified). Operational hypothesis validated for this verification; unverified as a physical claim.

Theorem T2 (exact). \(S(l)=\frac c6\log_2\!\big[(2n/\pi)\sin(\pi l/n)\big]+s_0\), \(qg_{ZZ}^c=-4C_{ij}^2\) and \(c_{\text{fit}}(n)\to1\) (exact).

Circuit design. An open XX chain of \(n=8\) sites at half filling and even blocks (exact). Real API calls: QangPhi.from_complex, qga.azimuth_qga, qecho.invert_readout, qang.qfalsify.decide/binom_upper, geometric_phase_qg and pancharatnam_phase (exact; tools/verify_exp_f2.py).
