Chapter 45 — Noise Benchmark Across Five Stacks: the qga Phase Survives qecho and Sector Filtering

Día de análisis y actualización: 24 de noviembre de 2026

Analysis and update date: November 24, 2026

Abstract. Does the phase remain measurable when five quantum stacks see the same state under depolarizing noise, amplitude damping and readout error? The witness is \(|\psi\rangle=(|0\rangle+e^{i\varphi}|1\rangle)/\sqrt2\) with \(\varphi=250\) qga (\(\pi/2\) rad), 65_536 shots and 600 estimates. exact: \(\sigma=0.621699\) qga, \(\delta_{5\sigma}=3.108\) qga, affine inversion recovers \(250.0\) qga and the sector filter keeps \(0.4\) of the distribution. simulado: raw readout under depolarizing noise gives \(243.24302\) qga (error \(6.75698\) qga, above \(5\sigma\)), 600/600 hits and the +50 qga control refuted 0/600; with inversion the MSE drops from \(1615.234375\) to \(19.369143\ \mathrm{m\text{-}qg}^2\) (\(83.39\times\)). Qiskit 2.6.2, PennyLane 0.45.1 and Cirq 1.7.0 provide real statevectors; QuTiP, Braket and IonQ are absent in this environment and are marked unverified. No measured gain and no QPU run. Verification: tools/verify_exp_f13.py.

45.1 Reading rules

  1. Units: \(\varphi\) in qga (1 turn \(=1000\) qga), azimuth \(\operatorname{atan2}(qg_Y,qg_X)\cdot1000/(2\pi)\); readout biases and variances in m-qg.
  2. Every number carries a label: exact if it comes from a formula, simulado if it comes from deterministic sampling with seed 21, unverified if it depends on an absent SDK or on hardware not executed.
  3. 5\sigma is an angular tolerance per estimate, not a p-value. The decision uses exact binomial tails with \(\alpha=0.05\) and \(p_0=2\delta/1000\) in qang.qfalsify.binom_upper.
  4. The hardware claim remains unverified: three statevectors are real, three are analytic fallbacks, and no QPU was queried.

45.2 Hypothesis, theorem and preregistration

H13 (unverified). With \(N=65\,536\) shots, combining qang.qecho.invert_readout and the sector filter lowers the angular error versus raw readout under three channels: depolarizing \(p=0.02\), amplitude damping \(\gamma=0.05\) and readout error \(e_{01}=0.02\), \(e_{10}=0.06\).

T13 (exact). For the equatorial state, \[ (qg_X,qg_Y,qg_Z)=(\cos\varphi,\sin\varphi,0), \] depolarizing scales by \(1-p\), amplitude damping sends \((x,y,z)\to(\sqrt{1-\gamma}x,\sqrt{1-\gamma}y,\gamma+(1-\gamma)z)\), and the affine readout \(m'=km+1000(e_{10}-e_{01})\) with \(k=1-e_{01}-e_{10}=0.92\) inverts as \(\hat m=(m'-40)/0.92\). Because X and Y undergo the same transformation, qang.qga.azimuth_qga recovers \(\varphi\) after inversion even when the raw azimuth shifts beyond \(5\sigma\).

Resolution (exact). With qang.qga.FISHER_PER_QGA2 \(=(2\pi/1000)^2\), \[ \sigma_{\mathrm{qga}}(N)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{NF}}=\frac{159.1549}{\sqrt N},\qquad \sigma(65\,536)=0.621699,\qquad \delta_{5\sigma}=3.108\ \mathrm{qga}. \]

Preregistration (exact). PREDICTION contains \(\varphi=250\) qga, the three channels, \(N=65\,536\), \(N_{\mathrm{cal}}=65\,536\), 600 estimates, seed 21, control +50 qga and the criterion: corrected azimuth within \(10^{-3}\) qga, raw error above \(5\sigma\), 600/600 hits, control refuted and inverted MSE below raw MSE in all three channels. qang.qfalsify.register gives ecb0dad97b5fa18e… and verify_registration is checked at startup.

45.3 Witness circuit and noisy model

import math
from qang.geometric import bloch_from_state
from qang.qecho import invert_readout, mse_raw_vs_inverted, readout_affine
from qang.qga import QGA_PER_TURN, azimuth_qga, radians_from_qga
from qang.sectors import filter_distribution

phi = radians_from_qga(250.0)
state = complex(math.cos(phi), math.sin(phi)) / math.sqrt(2)
bloch = bloch_from_state([1 / math.sqrt(2), state])
m = readout_affine(0.0, 0.02, 0.06)
print(round(invert_readout(m, 0.02, 0.06), 9))
print(round(azimuth_qga(0.0, 980.0), 5))
print(mse_raw_vs_inverted(0.0, 0.02, 0.06, 65536, 65536))
print(filter_distribution([0.40, 0.20, 0.20, 0.20], 2, 1))

The state is prepared with H and RZ(phi) in the stacks with real statevectors. Depolarizing and amplitude damping are applied to the Bloch vector and the affine readout to each \(qg_X,qg_Y,qg_Z\) component. The sector filter acts on a 2-qubit distribution with weights \((0.40,0.20,0.20,0.20)\): it keeps states with Hamming weight 1, which are \(0.4\) of the probability (exact) and \(0.402374268\) of 65_536 samples (simulado).

45.6 Limits and future directions

Limits. (i) The noise is an analytic one-qubit model, not device characterization; qang.qecho.invert_readout corrects the affine part, not coherences or temporal correlations. (ii) Amplitude damping preserves the equatorial azimuth but shortens the Bloch vector; the same is not claimed near a pole. (iii) QuTiP, Braket and IonQ are not installed here; their rows are unverified and tools/verify_exp_f13.py must be rerun with the extras. (iv) An IonQ QPU was not invoked: qiskit-ionq is an optional extra, not evidence.

Future directions, without result. Repeat with 5 and 20 qubits, with \(p\) and \(\gamma\) estimated by real characterization; compare against nonlinear readout mitigation; extend the sector filter to subspaces with weight \(w>1\); and measure on hardware with same-day calibration. None of these claims is exact today.
