Chapter 41 — Time Travel Read with qang: the Information Balance of Postselection, the Grandfather Paradox and Fixed Points by Angle

Updated: November 16, 2026

New chapter, continuing Ch. 17 (P-CTC versus D-CTC with three interactions) without repeating it. Module: qang.qtimeloop (exact NumPy). Verification: tools/verify_cap41.py. Status: exact for the maps; the physical existence of closed timelike curves is unverified and is not claimed: maps are simulated, not time machines. No hardware.

Abstract. For a postselected CTC with \(C=2\,\mathrm{diag}(1,a)\), the map \(\tan(\theta'/2)=a\tan(\theta/2)\) amplifies angular distinguishability by up to \(1/a\) at the south pole, but the success probability there is \(a^2\). Counting the success statistics, the total Fisher information per input copy is exactly \(1\) for every \((a,\theta)\) (max deviation \(8.5\cdot10^{-13}\) over 2000 random cases): the nonlinearity creates no information. At \(a=0.5\): amplification 2, \(p_\mathrm{post}=0.25\), ×4 shots. The grandfather paradox (\(U=I\otimes X\)) has \(p_\mathrm{post}=0\) for P-CTC and the zero-reading fixed point (0, 0, 0 m-qg) for D-CTC, with the CR output equal to its input. A rotation inside the loop has a 3-dimensional fixed set only when the angle is a whole number of turns (0 or 1000 qga) and a 1-dimensional one otherwise (250, 500 qga). Maps only; no claim about physical CTCs.

41.1 Reading rules

  1. Maps are simulated. Nothing here claims that closed timelike curves exist.
  2. qga is not m-qg. The loop rotation angles are in qga (1000 = one turn); the readings, in m-qg.
  3. An advantage per shot is not an advantage per copy. Every postselection figure is accounted with its success probability.

41.2 What it adds to the nonlinearity: the information balance

Let \(U=|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes I+|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes R_y(\alpha)\). Then \(C=\mathrm{Tr}_\mathrm{CTC}U=2\,\mathrm{diag}(1,a)\) with \(a=\cos(\alpha/2)\), and the P-CTC map on the polar angle is

\[ \tan\frac{\theta'}2=a\tan\frac\theta2,\qquad p_\mathrm{post}=\cos^2\frac\theta2+a^2\sin^2\frac\theta2,\qquad \frac{d\theta'}{d\theta}=\frac{a}{p_\mathrm{post}} . \]

Near the south pole (\(\theta=\pi\)) the amplification is \(1/a\): two nearly equal states end up more separated at the output. But there \(p_\mathrm{post}=a^2\). For \(a=0.5\): amplification 2, \(p_\mathrm{post}=0.25\), ×4 shots per success (pctc_amplification, pctc_p_post).

Exact result. For a family of pure states, the Fisher information per accepted shot is \((d\theta'/d\theta)^2\) and that of the success rate is \((dp/d\theta)^2/[p(1-p)]\). Their sum weighted per input copy is

\[ p\,\Big(\frac{d\theta'}{d\theta}\Big)^2+\frac{(1-a^2)\cos^2(\theta/2)}{p}=\frac{a^2+(1-a^2)\cos^2(\theta/2)}{p}=1 . \]

With 2000 random pairs \((a,\theta)\), \(|F_\mathrm{total}-1|\le 8.5\cdot10^{-13}\) (pctc_fisher). Postselection does not create information: what the amplified success gains, the failure rate loses. Only at the south pole does the success term equal 1 by itself (\(p\,F_\mathrm{acc}=1.0000\) with \(a=0.5\)); the rest of the circle splits the 1 between the success and the success probability.

41.3 The grandfather paradox

With \(U=I_\mathrm{CR}\otimes X_\mathrm{CTC}\) (the qubit that returns to the past is flipped): P-CTC gives \(C=\mathrm{Tr}_\mathrm{CTC}U=0\) and \(p_\mathrm{post}=0\): the history never occurs, there is no accepted shot to read. D-CTC solves the fixed point by iterating \(\rho_\mathrm{CTC}\leftarrow\mathrm{Tr}_\mathrm{CR}[U(\rho\otimes\rho_\mathrm{CTC})U^\dagger]\) from \(I/2\): the result is the state with readings \((0,0,0)\) m-qg (the fixed points of \(\rho\mapsto X\rho X\) are all those with \(qg_Y=qg_Z=0\); iteration from \(I/2\) picks the maximum-entropy one), and the chronology-respecting qubit leaves the same as it entered (deutsch_iterate; input \(qg_X=+1000\) m-qg, output \(+1000\) m-qg).

41.4 Fixed points of a rotation in the loop, by angle in qga

A rotation of angle \(\alpha\) (qga) about \(Z\) inside the loop fixes the Bloch vector of the CTC only if the vector is invariant: the set of fixed points is the kernel of \(R-I\) (bloch_rotation, fixed_dimension).

The period is that of qga (1000), not that of qg: only at a full turn does the loop not constrain the state.
