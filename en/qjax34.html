Rewrite of the limits chapter on the basis of Ch. 1–33. Module: qang.qlimits (exact NumPy). Verification: tools/verify_cap34.py. Summary: almost every limit has a common root: qg reads a Z-basis probability (\(p_0\)), and what is not in that probability it does not see. What follows proves it with code where possible, and flags as unverified what is only cited from the notes.

34.1 Reading rules

  1. Each limit enters with its function in qang.qlimits and its line in tools/verify_cap34.py. What comes only from the research notes (§) is marked unverified in this chapter: it was not re-run here.
  2. qga is the angular scale (1 turn = 1000 qga); m-qg is the reading \(1000\,qg_Z\). None of the limits below changes in passing from one to the other: they are limits on what is measured, not on how it is written.
  3. A negative result is not erased: it is recorded in Appendix H with its status.

34.2 Blindness of the Z basis: the graph state

A graph state is \(H^{\otimes n}\) followed by a CZ per edge. CZ is diagonal: it only changes phases. With the 6-qubit ring (ring_graph_state):

  • the Z distribution coincides with that of \(|+\rangle^{\otimes 6}\) with a difference of exactly 0;
  • \(qg_Z=0\) m-qg on each qubit and \(qg_S=1.00000\), the same as the product state;
  • \(\langle X_iZ_{i-1}Z_{i+1}\rangle=+1.0\) on the graph and \(+0.0\) on the product (stabilizer_expectation): they separate the two, but in the X basis.

Without qang the same happens: every Z-basis statistic, raw or tomographic, is blind to this entanglement. qang inherits the limit, it does not create it.

34.3 qgS is not monotone under T1 (but mean qgZ is)

Amplitude damping acts on the Z populations as a Markov chain \(1\to0\) with probability \(\gamma\) per qubit (damp_distribution); coherences do not change the Z distribution. With 24 random 4-qubit states and \(\gamma\in[0,0.5]\) (t1_noise_scan):

  • \(qg_S\) rises and falls in 24/24. Example (state 0): \(0.782\to0.847\) (\(\gamma=0.18\)) \(\to0.736\) (\(\gamma=0.50\));
  • the mean \(qg_Z\) grows monotonically in 24/24, and it is exact: each qubit goes from \(1-2P_1\) to \(1-2(1-\gamma)P_1\). In the example it goes from \(+251\) to \(+543\) m-qg;
  • therefore the pair (\(qg_S\), mean \(qg_Z\)) separates “little noise” from “a lot of \(T_1\)”, where \(qg_S\) alone cannot.

Honest scope. This result is for damping applied to the output state. In circuits with \(T_1\) between gates (quantum volume, notes §10.4 D) the mean \(qg_Z\) was monotone in 21–23 of 24 circuits: that number is cited, not re-run here (unverified).

34.4 The arccos range trap

\(\theta=\arccos(qg_Z)\in[0,\pi]\). If the bound of a minimum lies at \(\theta^*<0\), the update in \(qg\) cannot reach it. With \(E(\theta)=-\cos(\theta-\theta^*)\) and \(\theta^*=-0.5\) (best_cost_clipped):

minimum value where
unclipped (descent in \(\theta\)) \(-1.0000\) \(\theta=-0.5\)
with \(\theta\in[0,\pi]\) (via \(qg_Z\)) \(-0.8776\) \(\theta=0\)

Loss: \(0.1224\) of \(E\). The claims that “it converges in qg where \(\theta\) does not converge” came from this clipping. Direct optimization in \(qg_Z\) without regularization is not proposed.

34.5 Weight conservation: where the filter applies and where it does not

The sector filter requires every gate to conserve the Hamming weight. preserves_weight decides it on the unitary, and weight_breakers counts the guilty gates:

circuit (4 qubits) conserves weight? gates that break it
QFT no 4 (the Hadamards)
GHZ no 4 (H + 3 CNOT)
RBS ladder (full unitary) yes 0

The CPhase and SWAP gates of the QFT do conserve the weight; the Hadamards mix all weights. Phase estimation (QPE, and whatever builds on it) falls outside the filter.

34.6 Error inside the sector

The filter discards shots outside the sector; an error that conserves the weight passes entirely. With ideal RBS of \(\pi/2\) versus RBS\((\pi/2+0.3)\) (in_sector_error):

  • filter retention: 1.0 in both cases;
  • \(qg_Z\) of qubit 0: \(+1000.0\to+825.3\) m-qg: deviation of \(-174.7\) m-qg, invisible to the filter.

It is the same mechanism as in LiH (notes §50: the error is in the X/Y groups, out of reach of the Z filter). Those numbers are cited: LiH, 60 gates: 76.6 mHa raw versus 85.0 mHa with filter (unverified in this chapter).

34.7 The weight-1 network is a classical quadratic form

In the single-excitation sector, the RBS network acts as an orthogonal matrix \(O\) on the loaded data \(v\), and

\[qg_Z^{(i)}=1-2\,(Ov)_i^2 .\]

weight1_qgz evaluates it in \(O(n^2)\); simulate_qgz_weight1 simulates the full circuit (\(2^n\) amplitudes). With \(n=5\) and 20 random networks the maximum deviation is \(4.4\cdot10^{-16}\). What the filter protects exactly from \(T_1\) is, for that same reason, simulable. No quantum advantage is claimed.

34.13 A single readout basis: no-go, \(C_Z\) and purity

Every quantity computed only from the distribution \(P_Z\) shares the root of §34.2. Here the general case is closed, not just the graph. \(H_Z\) is in bits; \(H_Z/n\) is labeled separately and never enters \(C_Z=\sum_i H(X_i)-H_Z\).

Diagonal-twin theorem. For every \(p\), \(\sigma_p=\sum_x p(x)|x\rangle\langle x|\) is completely separable and has exactly that distribution. No rule that uses only \(p\) (with \(qg_Z\), \(qg_S\), \(H_Z\), \(C_Z\) or any learned summary) can certify entanglement, even with infinitely many shots (diagonal_twin). It costs less than it seems: it only takes a separable state that reproduces it.

With the same uniform \(P_Z\) (path_graph_state, local_purities, partial_transpose_min_eig):

state \(H_Z\) (bits) \(C_Z\) global \(P\) local \(P_i\) status
\(\lvert{+}{+}\rangle\) 2 0 1 1 product
graph \(G_2\) (edge) 2 0 1 0.5 entangled (PT min. \(-0.5\))
\(I_4/4\) 2 0 0.25 0.5 product mixture
\(\lvert{+}{+}{+}\rangle\) / graph \(G_3\) (path) / \(I_8/8\) 3 0 1 / 1 / 0.125 1 / 0.5 / 0.5 product / entangled / mixture

A bias in \(z\) does not remove it: \(\lvert a\rangle\lvert b\rangle\) and \(\mathrm{CZ}\lvert a\rangle\lvert b\rangle\), with \(z=(0.5,0.6)\), have \(P_Z=(0.60,0.15,0.20,0.05)\) and \(H_Z=1.533206\) bits; \(P_i=1\) versus \(0.76\); PT min. \(-0.346410\).

Purity. With the diagonal \(p\), the exact range of \(\mathrm{Tr}\rho^2\) is \([\sum_x p(x)^2,\,1]\); the extremes are \(\sigma_p\) and \(\lvert\psi_p\rangle=\sum_x\sqrt{p(x)}\lvert x\rangle\) (purity_range_from_z). The collision probability \(\sum p^2\) is purity only if \(\rho\) is diagonal in Z. For \(p=(0.6,0.15,0.2,0.05)\): \([0.4250,1]\).

\(C_Z\) is not entanglement, in both directions (total_correlation_z):

  • False negative: \(C_Z=0\) on the entangled graph state (edge and path).
  • False positive: \(\lvert\mathrm{GHZ}_3\rangle\) and the mixture \(\tfrac12(\lvert000\rangle\langle000\rvert+\lvert111\rangle\langle111\rvert)\) have the same \(P_Z\) and \(C_Z=2\) bits (the maximum); the mixture is separable (\(P=1/2\)).
  • Three-body dependence with independent pairs: the uniform mixture over even-parity strings has pairwise mutual information \(0\), \(H_Z=2\) and \(C_Z=1\) bit.
  • The result depends on the readout basis: \((I\otimes H)\lvert G_2\rangle=\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\) goes from \(H_Z=2\) to \(1\) and from \(C_Z=0\) to \(1\), with entanglement and purity intact.

\(H_Z=C_{\mathrm{rel},Z}=H_Z-S(\rho)\) only if \(\rho\) is pure (relative_entropy_coherence_z): in \(G_2\), \(2=2\); in \(I_4/4\), \(0\) versus \(H_Z=2\). And \(qg_S=h_2((1+qg_Z)/2)\) determines \(|qg_Z|\) but not its sign: it is not a second independent reading.

What does certify: a two-setting witness (witness_path, witness_radius). For the 3-qubit path, \(W'_3=2I-XZI-ZXZ-IZX\) takes the value \(-1\) on \(G_3\) and \(+2\) on the product and on \(I/8\). It is estimated with XZX (gives \(K_1\) and \(K_3\)) and ZXZ (gives \(K_2\)). With 1000 shots per setting and \(\delta=0.05\) the conservative radius is \(m\eta=0.293556\) (\(m=3\)): \(-1+0.2936<0\) certifies genuine tripartite entanglement. A non-negative value concludes nothing. The witness works for the 3-qubit and the 2-qubit path; it is not generalized to every graph.

Noise: two exact counterexamples to monotonicity. (i) Final dephasing \(\rho_t=(1-t)\lvert G_3\rangle\langle G_3\rvert+t\,I/8\): \(P_Z\) stays fixed and \(\langle W'_3\rangle=-1+3t\) (\(-1,\,0,\,+2\) at \(t=0,\,\tfrac13,\,1\)). (ii) Product under amplitude damping \(\gamma=0,\tfrac12,1\): \(H_Z=0,2,0\) bits and \(P=1,\tfrac14,1\) with \(C_Z=0\) always; neither entropy nor purity measures the amount of noise.

Shots. A mean in \([-1,1]\) with \(\varepsilon=0.05\) and \(\delta=0.05\) needs \(N=2952\) (Hoeffding; hoeffding_shots). For the entropy there is no analogous rule: the assumption-free bound (sufficient_shots_entropy, sufficient, not necessary, loose) gives \(N=133\,966\) (\(H_Z\), \(n=2\)), \(253\,199\) (\(H_Z\), \(n=3\)), \(1\,346\,071\) (\(C_Z\), \(n=2\)) and \(3\,973\,513\) (\(C_Z\), \(n=3\)). In addition, the plug-in \(C_Z\) is positive with uniform independent \(p\): bias \(\tfrac{2^n-1-n}{2N\ln 2}\) (plugin_cz_bias_uniform). With \(n=3\), \(N=100\) (1000 repetitions, seed 34): \(\hat H_Z=2.9478\), \(\hat C_Z=0.0307\) (theory \(0.0289\)) versus the exact \(3\) and \(0\); at \(N=10\,000\), \(0.0003\). It is statistical bias, not physical correlation.
