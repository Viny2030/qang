Chapter 40 — Matter and Antimatter Read with qang: Charge Conjugation, Zitterbewegung, Pair Creation and the Invisible Pair

Updated: November 16, 2026

New chapter, on the basis of Ch. 1–39 and Appendix G. Module: qang.qanti (exact NumPy, \(c=\hbar=1\)). Verification: tools/verify_cap40.py. Status: exact for everything computed; there is no hardware result and none of this is a new proposal about physics: they are standard results read in m-qg. What Appendix G already does (⟨β⟩ = cos θ_r, four-component CPT, the “Pythagoras qubit”) is not repeated here.

Abstract. On a 1+1 Dirac qubit \(H=p\sigma_x+m\sigma_z\), charge conjugation \(\psi\mapsto\sigma_y\psi^*\) maps energy \(+E\) to \(-E\) and flips all three readings \((qg_X,qg_Y,qg_Z)\): the antipodal map of the Bloch sphere, which no unitary implements. Hence no pure one-qubit state is its own antiparticle (max overlap \(7\cdot10^{-17}\) over 2000 random states): a Majorana particle needs a larger space. Zitterbewegung is a Rabi problem: \(qg_Z(t)=1/\gamma^2+\beta^2\cos2Et\) (mean 640.0, amplitude 360.0 m-qg at \(\gamma=1.25\); error \(4.5\cdot10^{-13}\)). Pair creation per mode is Landau–Zener, \(P=e^{-\pi m^2/eE}\) (numerical propagation agrees within \(2\cdot10^{-3}\)), read as \(qg=1-2P\). A spin singlet pair has local readings \(0\) and correlation \(-1000\) m-qg: it is invisible to single-qubit \(qg_Z\) (Chapter 34). Seeing the sign of \(\pm1000/\gamma\) at \(3\sigma\) costs \(N=9(\gamma^2-1)\) shots: 27 at \(\gamma=2\), 443 at \(\beta=0.99\). No hardware claim.

40.1 Reading rules

  1. qga is not m-qg. The angle is given in qga (1 turn = 1000 qga); the readings, in m-qg (\(1000\,qg_Z\)).
  2. The antiparticle is an inverted reading, not an added angle. \(C\) takes \(\theta\mapsto\pi-\theta\) at the pole-equator of the Dirac qubit and negates \(qg_X,qg_Y,qg_Z\) at once.
  3. What is claimed here is standard. Landau–Zener, Zitterbewegung and charge conjugation are known physics; qang contributes the reading scale and the shot budget.

40.2 Charge conjugation: the antipodal map

For \(H=p\sigma_x+m\sigma_z\), \(C\psi=\sigma_y\psi^*\) satisfies \(\sigma_yH^*\sigma_y=-H\), so it takes an energy eigenstate \(+E\) (\(E=\sqrt{p^2+m^2}\)) to one of \(-E\) (conjugate). On the qubit it acts on the three readings as \((qg_X,qg_Y,qg_Z)\mapsto-(qg_X,qg_Y,qg_Z)\): over 500 random eigenstates the error is \(1.1\cdot10^{-13}\) m-qg, and \(qg_Z=+1000\,m/E\) on the positive branch. That map is antiunitary: no gate implements it (the antipode of an unknown state is not obtained with a unitary). Exact consequence: \(|\langle\psi|C\psi\rangle|=0\) for every pure one-qubit state (maximum observed \(6.8\cdot10^{-17}\) over 2000 states), so no pure one-qubit state is its own antiparticle. A Majorana particle needs more space (Ch. 14, ex-11, Majorana phases).

40.3 Zitterbewegung as a Rabi problem

A state starting at \(|0\rangle\) (the pole, \(qg_Z=+1000\) m-qg) under \(H\) precesses around \(\hat n=(p,0,m)/E\) with frequency \(2E\):

\[ qg_Z(t)=\frac{m^2}{E^2}+\frac{p^2}{E^2}\cos 2Et=\frac1{\gamma^2}+\beta^2\cos2Et . \]

With \(p/m=0.75\) (\(\gamma=1.25\), \(\beta=0.6\)) the reading oscillates around \(640.0\) m-qg with amplitude \(360.0\) m-qg; the exact diagonalization (zb_qgz_mqg) agrees with the formula (zb_analytic_mqg) to \(4.5\cdot10^{-13}\) m-qg. It is the same geometry as the area rule of Ch. 2: the detuning is \(m\) and the coupling \(p\).

40.4 Pair creation: Landau–Zener

In a uniform field, each mode is an avoided crossing: with \(H=\tfrac{vt}{2}\sigma_z+\tfrac\Delta2\sigma_x\), the probability of staying in the diabatic state is \(e^{-\pi\Delta^2/2v}\) (lz_probability). With \(\Delta=2m\) and \(v=2eE\) it gives the Schwinger rate per mode, \(P=e^{-\pi m^2/eE}\) (pair_probability). Propagating the \(2\times2\) system between \(\pm250\) (lz_numeric), the agreement is \(2.0\cdot10^{-3}\) in the worst case (finite window). The vacuum/pair occupation is read as \(qg=1-2P\) (pair_reading_mqg).

40.5 The singlet pair is invisible in local readings

A particle–antiparticle pair in a spin singlet has \(qg_Z=0\) on each qubit (0.0 and 0.0 m-qg), but \(qg_{ZZ}=-1000\) m-qg (singlet_readings). It is the blindness of the \(Z\) basis of Ch. 34: the local reading does not see the pair; the correlation does.

40.6 How much it costs to see the sign

Distinguishing the \(+1000/\gamma\) branch from the \(-1000/\gamma\) one at \(z\) deviations from zero requires \(N=z^2(\gamma^2-1)\) shots (shots_to_certify_branch). At \(z=3\): 27 shots with \(\gamma=2\) and 443 with \(\gamma=7.089\) (\(\beta=0.99\)). The more relativistic the particle, the closer to the equator the reading sits and the more the branch costs.
